Architecture/03

Judgment from the model.
Cuts from code.

Claude, once per run

Decides

  • What story the stream tells, and what it promises the viewer.
  • Which moments become beats, and the job each one does.
  • Whether quest dialogue carries the plot or is filler to drop whole.
  • Whether the finished cut actually reads.

Deterministic, local

Executes

  • Exact in and out points, snapped so nothing ends mid-word.
  • Which silence is dead air, checked against a speech pass.
  • Captions, timestamps and the project manifest.
  • Caching, so a second cut of the same VOD skips the heavy work.

Expensive reasoning once, cheap local work many times. Transcription, chaptering and per-clip scoring stay on your machine with faster-whisper and ollama.

Features/04

Every rule came
from a bad cut.

[1204s]
[emphasis.py]

Captions that shout back

Word-by-word karaoke subtitles, lowercase by default and uppercase when the audio spikes.

mawikaMavuika
kinnichKinich
arlekinoArlecchino
[caption_fix.py]

Names, spelled right

One pass rereads the captions against the game glossary and the VOD's own chat. Accepted fixes are learned and reapplied free next time.

---
pace: 0.35	# 1.5-4s shots
snap: transient	# cut on the peak
stack: 8	# montage cold open
keep_build: false
captions: emph
---
Cut on the peak, not after it.
Never let a moment fully resolve.
[style.py]

Style as a document

Prose tells the director how to cut. Frontmatter sets the numbers prose can't reach. Any flag on the command line still wins.

239s → 24s
[silero vad]

Silence that wasn't

Speech the dead-air cutter deleted across seven real cuts, before silence was checked against a Silero speech pass instead of the transcript alone.

--stack 8
[pacing.py]

Cold opens that pay off

A montage of unexplained moments before beat one, best last. Each is footage a later beat actually delivers.

sentenceThe default. A cut never lands mid-sentence.
phraseLands on natural pauses for denser styles. Still never mid-word.
transientLands on the audio peak and leaves 0.3s before the joke resolves.
[--snap]

Cuts land where you choose

The snap mode is the real floor on shot length. The last cut of the video always keeps its sentence, so the ending never stops dead.

Usage/05

One command.

Only the VOD is required. Every flag moves a number the pipeline runs on, not an adjective in a prompt.

refire make 1762930614Any Twitch VOD id. Downloaded once, cached.
--brief "the gacha pulls and the rage"What the cut is about. Leave it out and the director finds the stream's own story.
--style ryukStyl.mdHow to cut it: free text, or a style document.
--duration 12mA target, not a cap. The cut may land within 35% of it.
--pace 0.8Below 1 means shorter shots and more beats.
--renderAlso renders an ffmpeg draft to rough.mp4.
